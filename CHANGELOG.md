# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-01

First public release of the Light Crimson theme for Pear Desktop.

- Light player, account menu, song menus, search suggestions, and Share dialog.
- Crimson progress bars, loading rings, and Premium download badge.
- Black player and queue text, with readable controls and Music wordmark.
- Restored artwork, video, avatar, and social sharing colours.
- Visible account-menu border and matching player-bar edge.
- Dark queue thumbnail overlays with white playback icons.
